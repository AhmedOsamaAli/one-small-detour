The cottage and articulated courier models, layered rose geometry, material
processing, atlas compositions and resulting renders were created for this
project. They are not Poly Haven preview images or an endorsed Poly Haven product.
Seasonal foliage is an artistic treatment of the licensed tree models; it is
not represented as a botanical scan of a different species.

Raw source meshes and high-resolution textures are offline baking inputs.
They are not loaded by the shipped game. Only the small baked atlases and
processed material maps in this folder are delivered.
