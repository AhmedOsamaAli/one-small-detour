One Small Detour - seasonal journey landscape art

The terrain, ponds, crop/lavender rows, windmill, lighthouse, snow treatment,
composition and resulting atlas renders were created for this project.
Ground and material detail reuse Poly Haven CC0-1.0 sources listed in:
  ../garden/LICENSES.txt

Source asset licence: https://polyhaven.com/license
CC0 licence: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Only the small baked atlases are used by the game. Raw scanned meshes and
high-resolution source textures are offline baking inputs, not runtime assets.
These are original compositions, not copied library preview renders.
